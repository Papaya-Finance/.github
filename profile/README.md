# Papaya

**Crypto subscriptions and recurring payments, on-chain.**

Papaya lets businesses accept recurring stablecoin payments across 10 EVM networks: subscriptions, payroll, pay-as-you-go, pay-per-view and agentic payments. Users keep custody of their funds and can cancel anytime.
